The first release of iContain: run apps inside one app on your iPhone, including builds made for the iOS Simulator.

**Features**
- Import an `.ipa`, `.xcarchive` or zipped `.app`, including Simulator builds, which iContain converts to run on the device
- Test mode: run an app as another iPhone or iPad, in light or dark mode
- Simulated location for apps in iContain, and for the whole device through LocalDevVPN
- More RAM: the Increased Memory Limit and Extended Virtual Addressing for your App IDs
- Sign with your Apple ID (anisette servers with Automatic mode) or a `.p12` development certificate
- `icontain://` links for Shortcuts
- Liquid Glass design on iOS 26 and later

**Install:** sign `iContain.ipa` with AltLoad, AltStore or SideStore. A `.p12` must be from the same team as the certificate iContain is signed with.

**Known limits:** app extensions and widgets, push notifications, running two apps at once, and the Keychain for apps with their own access group don't work yet.
