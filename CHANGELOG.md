## 1.0.0

First release.

- Run imported apps inside iContain: `.ipa`, `.xcarchive`, zipped `.app`, and builds made for the iOS Simulator
- Test mode: run an app as another iPhone or iPad, in light or dark mode
- Simulated location for apps in iContain, and for the whole device through LocalDevVPN
- More RAM: Increased Memory Limit and Extended Virtual Addressing for your App IDs
- Signing with an Apple ID (anisette servers with Automatic mode) or a `.p12` certificate
- `icontain://` links for Shortcuts
- Liquid Glass design on iOS 26 and later
