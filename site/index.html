05 / FAQ

Good to know.

Is it free?
Yes. iContain is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts or tracking.
Do I need a computer?
Only to sideload iContain the first time, if your sideloading tool needs one. AltLoad doesn't. Importing, signing and running apps all happen on your iPhone.
Does a free Apple ID work?
Yes. Apps inside iContain don't use up App IDs, because they run under iContain's own. A .p12 certificate works too, if it's from the same team that signed iContain.
Can I run App Store apps?
Only decrypted copies. App Store downloads are encrypted for the device they were installed on, and iContain tells you when an app is.
What doesn't work yet?
App extensions and widgets, push notifications, two apps at once, and the Keychain for apps with their own access group. iContain is at 0.1.
How do I get back to iContain?
Close the app from the app switcher, then open iContain again.
