Features

A simulator in your pocket.

Everything you'd open Xcode for, on the device you're actually holding.

Simulator builds on iPhone

Import a .app built for the iOS Simulator. iContain converts it to run on the real device.

Any build you have

.ipa, .xcarchive or a zipped .app, from Files, the share sheet or iContain's own folder.

Test as any device

iPhone SE to iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPad mini to iPad Pro, in light or dark mode, shown in a device frame.

Be anywhere

Pick a place on the map and apps in iContain see it. With LocalDevVPN, move the whole iPhone too.

More RAM

Switch on the Increased Memory Limit for your App IDs, so heavy apps and emulators stop getting closed.

No App ID limits

Every app shares iContain's one App ID. Sign with a free Apple ID or a .p12 certificate.

Location

Drop a pin. Be there.

Search for a place or tap the map. Apps running in iContain see that location, with nothing else to set up.

  • A different place for each app, or the same pin for all of them
  • Whole-device mode moves your iPhone's location for every app, the way Xcode does
  • Turn it off and everything goes back to your real location

FAQ

Questions

Is iContain free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts or tracking.

Do I need a computer?

Only to sideload iContain the first time, if your sideloading tool needs one. AltLoad doesn't. After that, importing, signing and running apps all happen on your iPhone.

Does it work with a free Apple ID?

Yes. Apps inside iContain don't use up App IDs, because they all run under iContain's own. You can also use a .p12 development certificate from the same team that signed iContain.

Can I run App Store apps?

Only decrypted copies. Apps downloaded from the App Store are encrypted for the device they were installed on, and iContain will tell you if an app is.

What doesn't work yet?

App extensions and widgets, push notifications, running two apps at once, and the Keychain for apps that rely on their own access group. iContain is at version 0.1, so expect rough edges.

How do I get back to iContain from an app?

Close the app from the app switcher, then open iContain again.
